PHILOSOPHY & MISSION ZERO-TELEMETRY • ANTI-DPI • HARDENED GATEWAY

Why was Dark Gateway OS created?

The modern internet is saturated with Deep Packet Inspection (DPI), pervasive censorship filters, browser fingerprinting, and ISP-level de-anonymization. Dark Gateway OS acts as an impenetrable security gateway, handling all encryption, routing, and filtering while keeping client workstations completely isolated.

🛡️

Zero-Leak Isolation

No application on client workstations can discover the real IP, MAC address, or geographic location of the host.

⚡

DPI Resistance

WebTunnel (HTTPS domain-fronting) and VLESS Reality bypass even the strictest national and corporate firewalls.

🚫

Clean Egress & Anti-Captcha

Routing through private exit nodes eliminates continuous Cloudflare/Google Captcha challenges.

Dark Gateway OS Desktop Panorama: Native C/GTK3 utilities, Conky telemetry HUD, and AwesomeWM tiling window manager.
